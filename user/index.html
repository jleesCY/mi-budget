The Complete User Guide

Every gesture, tool and setting in mi. Budget.

The Main Bar

mi. Budget opens on this month: its chart, the total you've spent and your purchases. Everything else is in one bar at the bottom of the screen, made for your thumb.

The + Button

Adds a purchase: choose Scan to read a receipt, or Add Manually to type it in.

Calendar

The button left of + opens the calendar.

The ⋮ Menu

Search and filters, the tools (the check splitter, currency converter and tip calculator), and links to your renewals and payment methods.

Library and Settings

The buttons at either end: your Library (subscriptions, budgets and tools) at the left, Settings at the right.

Gestures

Swipe Right

The Library slides in, following your finger.

Swipe Left

Settings slides in.

Swipe Up

The Calendar rises from the bar.

Hold

Search every purchase. Each page's own search Pill grows out of the bar's Pill, and swiping it back the way the page came returns you.

Change Month

Swipe the month title or the chart sideways.

Pro Tip: Forgot a swipe? Settings › About › Gesture Walkthrough takes you through each one, hands-on.

Purchases

Type the merchant and mi. Budget fills in the rest it can: the brand's logo, the category you used there last time, and the payment method you paid with.

Logos and Icons

About 950 brands are recognised as you type, even from bank-statement names like SQ *STARBUCKS. Tap the icon to choose a logo, up to 3 letters on a colour, a photo, or the category's icon.

Receipt and Notes

Attach a photo of the receipt and add a note. mi. asks before saving a purchase without a category.

A Purchase's Page

Tap a purchase to see it. The bar becomes its buttons: Delete, Refund and Edit (Delete and Save while editing). Swipe the bar right to go back, with the page following your finger. Subscriptions work the same way, with Delete and Edit.

Refunds

Refund a purchase in full or in part from its page, or swipe it right in the list. It stays in your history, crossed out, and only what wasn't refunded counts toward budgets and charts.

Swipe to Delete

Swipe a purchase left, or tap the bin on its page, to delete it. It goes at once, with Undo to bring it straight back, and it waits in Recently Deleted too.

Search and Filters

Search from the Pill, or filter by date (presets or your own range), category, uncategorized only, amount, expenses or refunds, where it came from (typed, scanned, subscription, imported), payment method and receipts, in any order.

Bulk Edit

Long-press a purchase to select several, then give them a category or delete them together, with Undo.

Charts

The chart at the top of the start screen shows where this month went. Under it: the total, the change from the same point last month, and how much of your budgets is left, with a safe amount to spend each day.

Five Styles

Donut, Pie, Rings, Bars or Trend. Tap the style button on the chart; your choice applies everywhere.

Drill Down

Tap a slice to see inside that category, and ↑ to go back up.

Touch and Hold

On the donut, pie and rings, hold a slice to preview it, then slide around the circle to preview the others. A light tick marks each change.

Shortcuts

Cards for Upcoming Renewals, Budgets and the Last 7 Days sit under the chart, with reminders for free trials and purchases that need a category.

Scanning

Scan opens Android's chooser: take a photo, or pick one from Photos or Files. Everything is read on your phone, and you always check the result before it's saved.

What It Reads

Receipts, banking-app screenshots and fuel pump or kiosk screens. It finds the merchant, total and date, and whether it's a purchase or a subscription (with its billing cycle and trial).

Share to mi. Budget

From any app, share a screenshot or photo to mi. Budget to scan it.

AI Scanners

Standard (Google's ML Kit) is built in. On supported phones, Settings › Scanner also offers Gemini Nano, which looks at the photo itself. After a scan, Rescan reads the photo again and Advanced Rescan reads it with Gemini Nano.

Free Receipts

A fully discounted receipt reads as $0.00, and $0 purchases can be saved.

Small photos: attached receipts are resized and saved as WebP, about 100 to 250 KB instead of 3 to 5 MB.

Calendar

Swipe the bar up for the month as a grid. Swipe sideways or use the arrows to change month; Today brings you back.

Each Day

Shaded by how much you spent, with the day's total, a count of its purchases and a ↻ mark on renewal days.

Tap a Day

See its purchases and renewals, or add a purchase on that date.

Subscriptions

In the Library: your monthly and yearly totals, a Coming Up row, and Active, Paused, Cancelled and All tabs.

Any Cycle

Weekly to yearly, or every N days, weeks, months or years. The next payment is worked out from the start date for you.

Auto-log

Each billing date becomes a purchase, so budgets stay right. A start date in the past fills in the payments since then.

Price, Fees and Taxes

Base price plus fees and taxes makes the total charged. Change any one and the others follow.

Price History

Add earlier periods with their own price and cycle (a student plan, say), and past payments use those prices. After a price change, choose Only Future to keep the past as it was.

Pause, Cancel, Resume

Stopping records the date. Resume On a Date restarts billing without rewriting what came before.

Reminders

A notification before each renewal, around 9 AM. Choose how many days ahead in Settings, or per subscription.

Free Trials

Nothing is billed, shown or logged during a trial, and paid billing starts on the day it ends. You're reminded at least 2 days before. When a trial ends, mi. asks: Keep It, or Cancel It.

Budgets & Categories

Categories are folders, as deep as you like. Each one shows its spending, its budget, the folders inside it, its subscriptions and recent purchases.

Budgets

Give any category a weekly, monthly, quarterly or yearly budget. A parent's budget counts everything inside it. Budgets says which are ahead of pace and which are over.

Defaults

19 categories to start with (55 with sub-categories). Add From Defaults brings back any you deleted.

Nothing Lost

Deleting a folder moves what was in it up a level.

Payment Methods

Save your cards, bank accounts and wallets once in Settings › Budgeting › Payment Methods, then pick them on any purchase or subscription.

Logos

84 bank, card and wallet logos, picked from the name. Or choose a symbol, letters or your own photo.

Remembered

A new purchase starts with the method you last used at that merchant. Filter purchases by method and see how much each is used.

Tools

In the ⋮ menu and the Library.

Check Splitter

Scan the bill to pull in every item, add people, and split evenly, by custom amounts or percentages, or by item (tap who had what; shared items split evenly). Rows like '5 Burgers' become one item each. Each person's Tip switch says who chips in for the tip, and every share rounds up to the cent. Share the result as text. Leave and come back to Continue where you were.

Tip Calculator

The bill, a 1 to 50% slider with shortcuts, and percent and dollar boxes that follow each other. Tip After Tax adds a tax box.

Currency Converter

About 160 currencies, built in, so it works with no connection. Add Daily Rate Updates in Settings › Tools to keep them current.

This Month Widget

On your home screen: spent so far, what's left of your monthly budgets and the next renewal.

On Your Home Screen

Long-press the app icon for Add Purchase, or drag it out as an icon of its own. Choose a Dark or Light icon in Settings › Look & Feel › App Icon.

Recently Deleted

Deleted purchases and subscriptions wait here for 30 days (or the time you choose) before they're gone. Restore any of them.

Backups

Settings › Backup & Reset › Back Up saves everything, receipt photos included, to a .mibudgetdata file you keep wherever you like. Restore it on any phone. Coming from Budgey? Export in Budgey (Settings › Your Data › Export), then restore it here.

Settings

Swipe the bar left for Settings. It opens on a list of sections; tap one to open its page, or search from the Pill to find any setting from any section. Here is every setting:

Pro Tip: Settings apply as you change them. Pick another accent and the whole colour scheme follows straight away.

Budgeting

Week Starts On

Sunday or Monday: where weeks start in the calendar and in weekly budgets.

Uncategorized Reminder

A banner on the start screen when purchases need a category.

Payment Methods

Your cards, bank accounts and wallets: a name, the last 4 digits if you like, and an icon.

Notifications

Renewal Reminders

A notification before subscriptions renew and free trials end. mi. asks for permission the first time you turn it on.

Remind Me

A slider from the same day to a week before a renewal. Each subscription can choose its own or turn reminders off. Free trials always get at least 2 days.

Scanner

Standard

The built-in reader (Google's ML Kit). Reads receipts, banking screenshots and pump screens on your phone. Nothing to download.

Gemini Nano

Google's on-device AI, built into Android on supported phones. Looks at the photo itself. Listed only where it works.

Tools

Built-in Rates

About 160 currencies of central-bank rates come with the app, so the converter works offline.

Daily Rate Updates

An optional 10 KB pack that keeps rates current, updating once a day when you're online.

Update Now

Get today's rates (with the update pack).

Look & Feel

Theme

Light, Dark or System.

Pure Black

True black backgrounds in dark mode.

Accent Color

Adaptive (your wallpaper's colours), Gray, Blue, Green, Red, Orange, Yellow or Purple. Green is mi. Budget's own.

App Icon

How mi. Budget looks on your home screen: the green wallet on Dark or Light, each shown as a preview.

Font Size

The app's text size, 80% to 120%.

Font

Standard, Rounded or your phone's System font.

Pan Overflowed Text

Text that doesn't fit slides to show the rest, in the places you choose: Settings, Purchases, Subscriptions, Budgets, Recently Deleted.

Corners

Square, Soft or Round.

List Spacing

Compact, Normal or Airy.

List Style

Cards or Table, for every list: purchases, subscriptions, budgets, payment methods and Recently Deleted.

Rows at Once

Long lists load 10, 25, 50 or 100 rows at a time, and more as you scroll, so they open smoothly.

Status Bar

Show or hide the system status bar.

Rotate Screen

Turn mi. Budget sideways with your phone.

Gesture Haptics

Light taps as gestures cross their thresholds.

Pill

Recent Searches

Your past searches appear above the Pill while you type.

Start Screen

Chart

How this month's spending is drawn: Donut, Pie, Rings, Bars or Trend.

Clock

A clock on the start screen: None, 12h or 24h.

Date

None, Above or Below the clock.
