# mi. Budget privacy policy

mi. Budget collects no personal data. There are no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no crash reporters, and mi. has
no servers of its own.

## What stays on your device

Everything you put in the app: purchases, refunds, subscriptions, categories and budgets, payment methods, receipt
pictures, your settings and your past searches. It's stored in the app's own storage on your phone and nowhere else.
Backups are files you export yourself (Settings › Backup & Reset) and keep wherever you choose. Deleted things wait in
Recently Deleted on the phone until they're purged. Receipts are read on the phone, including by the optional AI
models.

## Connections mi. makes

mi. Budget goes online only for these, each doing a job for you and sending nothing about you:

- **Update checks** (GitHub, `api.github.com`): looks for a newer version when the app opens (at most every 12 hours)
  and when you tap Check for Updates, and downloads the update if you choose to install it. It also fetches a new
  version's release notes for What's New.
- **Currency rates** (Frankfurter, `api.frankfurter.dev`): only if you add the optional daily update pack in
  Settings › Tools.

ML Kit's usage reporting is turned off, so text recognition sends nothing.

## Permissions

- **Notifications**: asked the first time you turn on renewal reminders (with a plain question first, then Android's
  prompt). Used only for the reminders you asked for.
- **Camera**: used through Android's own camera app when you scan a receipt; mi. Budget never opens the camera itself.
- **Internet**: for the connections above.
- **Install packages**: lets Android install an update you chose to install; Android asks you first.

## Children

mi. Budget isn't made for children and collects nothing from anyone.

## Changes and contact

Changes to this policy are made in this file, with the app's source, at https://github.com/mi-app-hub/mi-budget.
Questions or problems: open an issue there.
