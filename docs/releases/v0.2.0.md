# mi. 0.2.0: Budgey is now mi. Budget

Budgey has joined the mi. family. Everything you could do is still here, now around one bar at the bottom of
the screen: a big + scans a receipt or adds a purchase by hand, the calendar and a menu sit beside it, and your Library and Settings are at
either side. Swipe the bar right, left or up and those pages follow your finger in; hold it to search; swipe the
month to change it. You can also refund
purchases now, and deleted things wait in Recently Deleted for 30 days.

## New
- **Refunds:** refund a purchase in full or in part from its page, or swipe it right in the list. It stays
  in your history, crossed out, and only what wasn't refunded counts toward budgets and charts.
- **Swipe rows:** swipe a purchase left to delete it, with Undo.
- **Recently Deleted** in Settings › Your Data: restore anything deleted in the last 30 days.
- **This Month widget** for your home screen.
- **Hide From Screenshots** in Settings › Privacy.
- **Updates and What's New** right in the app (Settings › About).
- A short walkthrough of the Pill's gestures (replay it from Settings › About).

## Changed
- The mi. look: dark by default, mi. Budget green, seven accents or your wallpaper's colours, Pure Black,
  Material Symbols everywhere, and mi.'s dialogs, menus and fields.
- Settings works like every mi. app: Look & Feel, Pill, Start Screen, Gestures, Recently Deleted, Privacy &
  Security, Backup & Reset and About, with search in the Pill that finds any setting.
- New settings: Font Size, Pan Overflowed Text, List Spacing, List Style, Status Bar, Rotate Screen,
  Recent Searches, a Clock and Date on the start screen, Long Press to Search, Swipe Between Months, and how long
  Recently Deleted keeps things.
- Backups are `.mibudgetdata` files; Budgey's backups still import.
- **Charts:** small categories on the donut and pie are proper segments instead of dots and slivers, the
  smallest are grouped into "Other" when there are many, and each ring shows its share of all spending.

## Good to know
- mi. Budget is a new app, so it installs beside Budgey. Export a backup in Budgey and import it here to
  bring everything over.
