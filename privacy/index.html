Privacy Policy

Last updated November 24, 2026

mi. Budget collects no personal data. There are no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no crash reporters, and mi. has no servers of its own.

What stays on your device

Everything you put in the app: purchases, refunds, subscriptions, categories and budgets, payment methods, receipt pictures, your settings and your past searches. It's stored in the app's own storage on your phone and nowhere else.

Backups are files you make yourself (Settings → Backup & Reset › Back Up) and keep wherever you choose. Deleted things wait in Recently Deleted on the phone until they're purged. Receipts are read on the phone, including by Gemini Nano on phones that have it.

Connections mi. makes

mi. Budget goes online only for these, each doing a job for you and sending nothing about you:

  • Update checks (GitHub, api.github.com): looks for a newer version when the app opens (at most every 12 hours) and when you tap Check for Updates, and downloads the update if you choose to install it. It also fetches a new version's release notes for What's New.
  • Currency rates (Frankfurter, api.frankfurter.dev): only if you add the optional daily update pack in Settings → Tools.

ML Kit's usage reporting is turned off, so text recognition sends nothing. These services receive your IP address and basic device information, as with any web request, under their own privacy policies.

Permissions

  • Notifications: asked the first time you turn on renewal reminders (with a plain question first, then Android's prompt). Used only for the reminders you asked for.
  • Camera: used through Android's own camera app when you scan a receipt; mi. Budget never opens the camera itself.
  • Internet: for the connections above.
  • Install apps: lets Android install an update you chose to install; Android asks you first.

Children

mi. Budget isn't made for children and collects nothing from anyone.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date. Questions? Open an issue on GitHub.
