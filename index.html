Your money, at a glance.

mi. Budget shows you this month at a glance: what you've spent, where it went and what's left of your budgets. Scan a receipt or type a purchase in, swipe between months, and keep an eye on subscriptions and free trials before they charge you. Everything stays on your phone: no accounts, no ads, no tracking.

Interaction

One bar. Everything in reach.

A big + adds a purchase. Beside it are the calendar and a menu for search, filters and the tools, with your Library at the left and Settings at the right. Swipe the bar and those pages follow your finger in. Open a purchase and the bar becomes its own buttons.

  • Swipe right for the Library, left for Settings
  • Swipe up for the Calendar
  • Hold to search every purchase
  • Swipe the month sideways to change it
  • On a purchase: Delete, Refund and Edit; swipe right to go back
Insight

Charts that read at any size.

Donut, pie, rings, bars or trend. Small categories still get a proper segment, tapping a slice drills into it, and touching and holding previews it while you slide around the circle.

  • Five chart styles, chosen once for the whole app
  • Change from last month and a safe daily amount
  • Drill down through nested categories
  • Haptic ticks as you slide between slices
Scanning

Scan it. Check it. Done.

Snap a receipt, or share a banking screenshot or a pump screen from any app. mi. Budget reads the merchant, total and date on your phone, tells a purchase from a subscription, and opens an editor ready to check.

  • Receipts, banking screenshots and pump screens
  • Merchant logos for about 950 brands
  • On-device AI with Gemini Nano, on supported phones
  • Photos kept small: about 100 to 250 KB each
Subscriptions

No more surprise charges.

See what your subscriptions cost each month and year, what renews next, and which free trials are about to start charging. Reminders arrive before the money goes, and every payment can log itself.

  • Renewal reminders and free-trial warnings
  • Payments logged automatically
  • Price history, pause, cancel and resume
  • Weekly to yearly, or every N days
Budgets

Budgets that roll up.

Categories are folders you can nest as deep as you like. Give any of them a weekly, monthly, quarterly or yearly budget, and a parent's budget counts everything inside it.

  • 19 categories to start with, 55 with sub-categories
  • Ahead of pace and over budget, said in words
  • Refunds only count what you kept
  • Deleting a folder moves its contents up
Tools

The little things, handled.

Scan a restaurant bill and split it by item, work out the tip, or convert a price in about 160 currencies without a connection. Refund what you return, undo what you delete, and keep your backups as files of your own.
